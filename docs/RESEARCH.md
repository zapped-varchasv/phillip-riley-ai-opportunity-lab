## Proposed process map

The following is an inference to validate with staff:

| Stage | Likely work | Candidate trial | Discovery question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account development | Learn about projects and client needs | Source-backed account brief | How is a project signal checked and turned into a conversation? |
| Role intake | Agree requirements and working conditions | Brief completeness and draft | Which facts are frequently missing or re-entered? |
| Talent engagement | Discuss roles and record candidate context | Structured notes and follow-up drafts | Where is the trusted status and communication permission recorded? |
| Client delivery | Present progress and coordinate interviews | Weekly progress brief | How are stage counts and reporting periods defined? |
| Placement and workforce support | Hand over to onboarding and administration | Checklist or exception review | Who owns each handover and final approval? |
| Management review | Understand activity, service and cost | Tool usage and renewal review | Which capabilities are paid for, configured and used? |

This is not a statement that PRG operates in this exact sequence or that its records are incomplete.

## Assumption register

| ID | Assumption | How to replace it with evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A01 | Drafting and reporting are worthwhile first trials | Shadow staff and time current tasks |
| A02 | Existing capabilities may cover these needs | Review licence entitlements and demonstrate the configured features |
| A03 | Staff will adopt the improved workflow | Observe supervised use and ask for feedback |
| A04 | Trial volume and review burden support value | Record actual volume, gross saving, corrections and final review time |
| A05 | Current cost inputs are useful illustrations | Obtain like-for-like quotes and internal implementation estimates |
| A06 | A small template demo can clarify requirements | Ask staff to challenge output, missing fields and approval stages |
| A07 | Workflow ownership can be assigned | Confirm sponsor, process owner, data owner and support owner |

## Research conclusion

The strongest evidence supports investigating simple workflow assistance and existing vendor capabilities. It does not support a conclusion that PRG needs a custom AI platform, has a specific inefficiency, or will achieve the modelled savings. The first recommendation is a structured discovery and baseline exercise.
